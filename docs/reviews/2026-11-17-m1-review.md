## Live-run notes

Behaviour of the clinic agent seen in live runs. None of it is a Parakh defect.

- The agent booked wrong slots, and in the final run it booked a wrong phone number while reading back the right one. Loose WER cannot catch this, because the spoken words match the script. M4 end-state task success (compare the booking store with the scenario's expected end state) is the check for it.
